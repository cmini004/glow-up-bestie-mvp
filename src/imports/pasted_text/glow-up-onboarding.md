Design a **mobile-first onboarding experience for “Glow Up Bestie”**, an AI accountability bestie for women who want to become consistent with their gym and glow-up goals.

The onboarding should feel like a **personal transformation journey**, not a traditional fitness app signup.

## CORE PSYCHOLOGY

Use ethical sales psychology throughout the onboarding:

* Identity-based motivation
* Future pacing
* Emotional identification
* Problem awareness
* Self-reflection
* Micro-commitments
* Consistency bias
* Goal commitment
* Loss aversion without shame
* Personalization
* Progress momentum
* Clear value before asking for signup

The user should progressively feel:

**“I want to change” → “I understand why I keep failing” → “I can actually do this” → “I want accountability” → “I'm ready to commit.”**

Do NOT use manipulative guilt, body shaming, unrealistic transformation promises, or fake urgency.

# VISUAL DESIGN SYSTEM

## Overall aesthetic

Premium feminine wellness startup.

Think:

**clean + aspirational + emotionally warm + slightly editorial**

NOT:

* childish
* overly pink
* generic gym app
* aggressive bodybuilding aesthetic
* cliché stock photos
* excessive emojis

## Colors

Primary:

Soft blush / rose

Background:

Warm cream / off-white

Text:

Deep charcoal

Secondary:

Muted mauve

Success:

Soft green

Error:

Muted red

Use color sparingly.

## Typography

Use a modern sans-serif such as **Inter** or **DM Sans**.

Large headlines:

32–40px

Body:

16–18px

Supporting text:

14–16px

Buttons:

16px semibold

## UI

* Rounded cards
* 16–24px corner radius
* Large tap targets
* Generous whitespace
* Minimal navigation
* One primary CTA per screen
* Subtle transitions
* Progress indicator at top

## Progress indicator

Use a subtle progress bar:

**●━━━━━━━━**

It should communicate progress without making the user feel trapped in a long form.

# INTERACTION PRINCIPLES

Every screen should answer:

**Why should I care?**

before:

**What do you need from me?**

Every question should feel like it contributes to the user's personalized experience.

Avoid asking for information that isn't needed for the MVP.

Use microcopy to maintain momentum.

Examples:

**“Be honest.”**

**“This is your commitment.”**

**“Choose what you can actually keep.”**

**“You don't need to be perfect.”**

**“One bad day doesn't erase your progress.”**

---

# IMPORTANT PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

The onboarding should NOT promise:

**“We'll transform your body in 90 days.”**

The promise is:

**“We'll help you become more consistent.”**

The transformation is the consequence of consistency.

The emotional promise of Glow Up Bestie is:

> **“You don't have to become motivated every day. You just have to keep showing up.”**
