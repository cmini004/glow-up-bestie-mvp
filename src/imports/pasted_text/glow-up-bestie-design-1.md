Design a polished mobile-first SaaS/web app called “Glow Up Bestie.”

PRODUCT
Glow Up Bestie is an AI accountability companion for women who want to consistently follow through on their gym and fitness goals.

CORE PROBLEM
Women often know what they want to do but repeatedly fall off when motivation disappears or life gets in the way. Existing fitness apps provide workouts, plans, and tracking, but Glow Up Bestie focuses on proactive accountability and helping users recover after missed commitments.

CORE USER OUTCOME
“I actually show up for the commitments I make to myself.”

CORE PRODUCT LOOP
Make a commitment → Glow Up Bestie remembers it → proactive check-in → user shows up → user checks in/proves completion → AI reinforces progress → if user misses, AI helps her recover → repeat.

IMPORTANT PRODUCT PRINCIPLE
DO NOT design this as a traditional fitness app.

Do NOT make the primary experience:
- workout libraries
- calorie tracking
- exercise tracking
- complicated analytics
- social feeds
- leaderboards
- wearable integrations

The product should feel like a supportive, proactive AI best friend/accountability partner.

The emotional feeling should be:
“Someone remembers what I said I wanted and is helping me become the person I said I wanted to be.”

DESIGN DIRECTION
Create a premium Gen-Z feminine wellness aesthetic.

Mood:
- confident
- warm
- aspirational
- motivating
- playful
- emotionally supportive
- modern
- slightly editorial
- premium but approachable

Avoid:
- childish “girly” design
- overly pink fitness-app clichés
- aggressive gym-bro aesthetics
- generic SaaS dashboards
- excessive gradients
- clutter
- gamification overload

USER MAKES A PROMISE

PRODUCT REMEMBERS
↓
PRODUCT CHECKS IN
↓
USER SHOWS UP

PRODUCT REINFORCES
